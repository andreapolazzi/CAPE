Sink Files

gsm_gapfilled_sink.csv

A sink obtained by Rhodococcus opacus PD630 Genome Scale Model, with dead-end metabolites being removed. It is the most suitable sink for most applications.

biocyc_cmpd_rpcleaned.csv

A sink obtained by quering BioCyc for R. opacus metabolites (PD630 was not available). It is useful for increasing the possibility of merging RetroPath2.0 output with the KEGG network. Even though in most cases it would not be relevant, it is worth noting that not all compounds here are guaranteed to be actually present in PD630, and some compounds in PD630 might not be present in the sink.

kegg_rp.csv

A sink obtained by considering all compounds in our KEGG network, including those of "white" edges and therefore not present in R. opacus PD630. When no other solution can be found with other types of sink, this one can offer a way to dramatically increase the chance to merge the networks, as reaching a sink compound here intrinsically means to merge the graphs.
