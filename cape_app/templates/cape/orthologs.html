Orthologs Analysis Results

For each EC number required by the selected pathway but not encoded in the genome of Rhodococcus opacus PD630, CAPE expands the search to orthologous organisms using the NCBI Protein database. The API-driven search widens stepwise—from closely related taxa to broader clades—until reviewed (Swiss-Prot) or unreviewed entries are found, reaching across the Bacteria domain if needed.

The resulting protein sequences can be refined using length filters, sorted according to various criteria, and selected individually or in groups when multiple subunits are involved. Sorting by frequency prioritizes organisms that recur most often in the search results so you can focus on commonly represented candidates.

You can also enter your custom protein sequences.
