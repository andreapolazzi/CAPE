Optimize Codon Usage

In this step, CAPE adapts all protein-coding sequences to the codon preferences of Rhodococcus opacus PD630 to enhance transcription efficiency, translation accuracy, and overall protein yield.

Codon optimization is performed using the codon usage table for R. opacus, with two selectable modes:

  • Max mode — a deterministic approach that replaces each amino acid with the most frequently used codon in R. opacus.
  • Weighted mode — a probabilistic method that samples codons according to their natural usage frequencies. This mode accepts a random seed to ensure reproducibility across runs.

Additionally, CAPE includes a restriction site screening module, which detects and removes sites incompatible with downstream cloning and assembly standards. Users can enable this option and select from predefined or custom restriction site lists. Available presets include:

  • Type IIS RFC1000 — removes BsaI and SapI sites.
  • BioBrick RFC10 — removes EcoRI, XbaI, SpeI, PstI, and NotI sites.

You can also specify custom restriction sites by entering their recognition sequences, separated by commas: e.g., GAATTC,AAGCTT,GGATCC.
